SpectrumStep – Multi-Space Gradient & Contrast Generator

Create precise discrete color gradients and evaluate text contrast in real time across HSL, HSV, and RGB color spaces.

How to use SpectrumStep

1. Configure Colors: Toggle between HSL, HSV, and RGB spaces for each color picker. Adjust sliders or fine-tune components directly using numeric inputs.

2. Invert & Copy: Invert color channels using the RGB-Invert, HSL-Invert, or copy colors from side to side.

3. Set Steps & Overdrive: Define the exact number of discrete gradient steps and use the Overdrive for outer colors.

4. Color Spaces & Contrast: Switch the interpolation space (HSL, RGB, HSV) and test legibility with custom light, dark, or user-defined background and text combinations.

5. Export & Copy: Click any color step in the palette to copy its HEX value instantly, or copy the ready-to-use linear-gradient CSS code snippet generated at the bottom.

What is HSL?

HSL stands for Hue, Saturation, and Lightness and is often also called HLS. Hue defines the color, Saturation determines its intensity, and Lightness controls its brightness, ranging from black to white. HSV stands for hue, saturation, and value, and is also often called HSB. See wikipedia: HSL and HSV
