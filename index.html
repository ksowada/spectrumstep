HSL Color Mixer

Mix two colors to create custom color palettes and smooth color transitions. Enter colors as HEX or HSL values, adjust the number of steps, and choose between HSL and RGB interpolation. Use hue inversion and Overdrive to explore additional color variations.

What is HSL?

HSL stands for Hue, Saturation, and Lightness. Hue defines the color, Saturation its intensity, and Lightness its brightness, ranging from black to white.

How to use the HSL Color Mixer

  • C1 and C2: Enter HEX or HSL values, or select colors using the color picker.
  • Steps: Set the number of intermediate colors in your palette.
  • HSL or RGB: Choose the color space for interpolation.
  • Hue inversion: Reverse the hue direction to create alternative color transitions.
  • Overdrive: Extend the color range beyond the two base colors.
  • Text contrast: Explore text and background color combinations.
  • Light and dark mode: Invert colors to explore alternative color schemes.
  • Save and load: Store your color selections locally in your browser without cookies.
